Public Pool Page

Generate an embeddable pool card for a website you already run. Sidekick hosts nothing and opens no public route — the card reads live data from public APIs at view time.

No public surface on this app. Sidekick stays loopback-bound. This screen produces a snippet you paste onto your own site; at view time it queries only public, unauthenticated on-chain data keyed by your manager principal. Your gas key, job internals, and alerts are never part of it.
